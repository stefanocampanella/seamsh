Changes made in this fork (https://github.com/stefanocampanella/seamsh)
-----------------------------------------

The fork branched from upstream's version 0.4.18. Its changes are by Stefano Campanella, and are
recorded in the git history and in CHANGELOG.rst.

- Changes to existing files are licensed under the same terms as the files they are in. Code
  moved out of an existing file into a new one keeps the terms of that file: seamsh/_libseamsh.py,
  seamsh/_projection.py and seamsh/_progress.py, split from upstream's seamsh/_tools.py, are under
  the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later, with upstream's copyright.
- Files added by the fork are licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your
  option) any later version (COPYING.txt), so that the project as a whole is under one license.

The copyright and license of every file are also given in machine-readable form, following the
REUSE specification (https://reuse.software): SPDX headers in the files that take comments, and
REUSE.toml for the others (and for upstream's and third-party files, whose headers are kept as
they are); the license texts are in LICENSES/. `reuse lint` checks them.

Files from other projects
-----------------------------------------

In seamshlib:

- polymesh.c: a C version of PolyMesh (Mesh/meshPolyMesh.h) from Gmsh, Copyright (C) 1997-2021
  C. Geuzaine, J.-F. Remacle, GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, with the exception
  stated in Gmsh's LICENSE.txt. A copy of Gmsh's LICENSE.txt, as of Gmsh 4.8.4 (2021), is in
  seamshlib/LICENSE.gmsh.txt.
- polymesh.h: the C interface of polymesh.c. It has no header and its license is not stated; it is
  distributed with polymesh.c, under the same terms.
- robustPredicates.c, robustPredicates.h: the robust geometric predicates of Jonathan Richard
  Shewchuk (placed in the public domain), as modified in Gmsh (static filters set by exactinit):
  robustPredicates.c is Gmsh 4.8.4's Numeric/robustPredicates.cpp with three lines changed. The
  modifications are distributed with Gmsh, under Gmsh's license (above).
- qsort.h: Copyright (c) 2013, 2017 Alexey Tourbin, MIT license (text in the file).
- vector.h: from MigFlow, Copyright (C) 2010-2020 Universite catholique de Louvain and Universite
  de Montpellier, GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later.

In tests/data:

- ne_110m_coastline: the 1:110m coastline of Natural Earth, version 4.1.0
  (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), in the public domain.

seamsh as a whole
-----------------------------------------

The library compiled from seamshlib (seamsh/libseamsh.so) contains the files from Gmsh above, and
the Python package cannot be used without it. seamsh as a whole (this source tree, and packages
or images that contain the compiled library) is therefore distributed under the terms of the GNU
General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version (COPYING.txt). Both
licenses allow it: the GNU Lesser General Public License of seamsh's own files is the GNU General
Public License with additional permissions, and the license of the files from Gmsh is the GNU
General Public License, version 2 or later. The files of seamsh itself can be used
separately under the GNU Lesser General Public License, as their headers say.
